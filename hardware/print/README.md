# Print and fold

The Studio build sheet prints the track at 1:1 on tiled pages. This folder is
for the physical recipe.

## Card track

1. Print the sheets at 100 percent on 160 to 200 gsm card.
2. Check the 1 inch test square with a ruler. If it is off, fix the printer
   settings before cutting.
3. Glue the sheets to 1.5 mm greyboard. A glue stick plus a roller works.
4. Cut the outside edge and the slot. Cut the slot first, then the outside.
5. Fold the rail strip or lay tape. Test a short section with a car before
   committing to the full layout.
6. Label the back with the piece id and layout name.

## Folding

- Score fold lines with a blunt knife before folding.
- Leave a 5 mm glue tab on straight sections.
- Do not fold across a joint. Make each joint a separate flat piece.
- Card thickness changes the effective radius on the inside of a fold. Cut the
  slot after folding, not before.

## Material notes

- Plain paper is too flexible and the slot deforms.
- 1.5 mm greyboard is the sweet spot for HO.
- Foam board is easy to cut but the rails sink into the foam.
- MDF is the durable option, but then you are routing, not printing.

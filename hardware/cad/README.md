# CAD parts

Planned printable parts. Keep each part parametric and driven by the piece
library, not by hand measurements.

- Riser set for overpasses: 1, 2, 3 and 4 inch heights, with a track clip.
- Banked curve support for 9 and 12 inch radius curves.
- Card track joiner and slot alignment jig.
- Guard rail end cap.
- Power tap cover.
- Measuring jig for slot spacing and rail offset.

Use build123d or OpenSCAD. Export STL and STEP. Put the source next to the
export, and record the tested printer and material in a short note.

Dimensions to confirm before modelling:

- Section width and thickness
- Tab and slot depth at the joint
- Rail height and width
- Underside rib positions
